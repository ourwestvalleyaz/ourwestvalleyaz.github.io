Trash & disposal

Bulk / Uncontained Trash

Monthly bulk trash collection

Avondale provides monthly residential collection for large bulk items and green waste that do not fit in the regular trash container.

Find your collection week
Zone A 1st Monday week
Zone B 2nd Monday week
Zone C 3rd Monday week
Zone D 4th Monday week

Not sure which zone you're in? Use Avondale's official zone map and schedule.

Collection basics

  • Set bulk trash out by 5:00 a.m. Monday of your scheduled zone week.
  • Crews may collect anytime Monday through Friday.
  • Materials may be placed out no more than 24 hours before the scheduled Monday.
  • Bulk and green waste have a combined limit of 3 cubic yards per household per month.
  • Place material in the street against the curb. Bulk placed on private property cannot be serviced.
  • Green waste and bulk trash do not need to be separated.

Household Hazardous Waste

Household hazardous waste events

Avondale holds household hazardous waste collection events twice each year, once in the spring and once in the fall.

Check the next event

Event dates, hours, locations, and accepted materials can change. Check Avondale's official page for the next scheduled event before loading or transporting materials.

Who can use the service?

  • The service is free to Avondale residents.
  • Proof of Avondale residency is required.
  • Residential hazardous waste is accepted. Commercial waste is not.

Examples of accepted materials

The city's accepted-material list includes items such as motor oil, batteries, paints and related materials, household chemicals, electronics, propane tanks, fluorescent tubes, and certain tires.

This is only a summary. Always check the city's current accepted and prohibited material lists before attending.
