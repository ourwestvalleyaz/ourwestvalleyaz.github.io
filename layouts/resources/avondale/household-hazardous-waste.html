Household Hazardous Waste

Household hazardous waste disposal

Household hazardous waste includes common household products that can be harmful to people, property, or the environment if placed in regular trash, recycling, or disposed of improperly.

Next HHW Collection Event

November 21, 2026

Time 8:00 a.m. – 12:00 p.m.
Location Avondale Civic Center Complex
Entrance Coldwater Springs Blvd.
Who Avondale residents only

Events are first-come, first-served. No appointment is required. Materials are not accepted after the event closing time.

Before you arrive

  • Keep liquids in original containers whenever possible.
  • Do not mix products together.
  • Ensure lids and caps are tightly sealed.
  • Wrap breakable containers to prevent leaks or breakage.
  • Place materials in a box in your trunk, cargo area, or truck bed.
  • Remove rims from tires before arriving.

At the event

  • Bring a valid photo ID or current utility bill showing Avondale residency.
  • Follow traffic directions and staff instructions.
  • Remain in your vehicle.
  • Event staff will unload and sort materials.

Lithium-ion battery collection station

Avondale now provides a collection station for small lithium-ion batteries that can be easily removed from household devices.

Location:
Recycling Drop-Off Center
Civic Center Library parking lot
11350 W Civic Center Drive

The station is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

HHW Reference

Materials & FAQs

What materials are accepted?
Automotive products

Motor oil, transmission fluid, antifreeze, brake fluid, fuel additives, lubricants, oil filters, automotive cleaners, and degreasers.

Paints & finishing products

Paints, primers, sealers, varnishes, lacquers, wood stains, paint removers, solvents, adhesives, and caulking.

Household chemicals & cleaners

Aerosols, rubbing alcohol, spot removers, rug and upholstery cleaners, shoe polish, nail polish and remover, correction fluids, and art and hobby supplies.

Lawn & garden chemicals

Fertilizers, herbicides, insecticides, fungicides, poisons, and bait.

Fuels & flammables

Gasoline, kerosene, propane tanks, charcoal lighter fluid, mineral spirits, and other flammable liquids.

Batteries & electronics
  • Household batteries, including alkaline, rechargeable, lithium-ion, and button-cell batteries
  • Vape pens and e-cigarettes
  • Computers, laptops, tablets, cell phones, printers, monitors, and televisions
  • Keyboards, mice, cables, chargers, power supplies, and other small electronic accessories
  • Items containing lithium-ion batteries such as power tools, electric toothbrushes, portable speakers, gaming devices, and battery-powered household equipment
Safety & specialty items

Fire extinguishers, photography chemicals, fluorescent tubes, CFL bulbs, smoke detectors, thermometers, and thermostats.

Tires

Passenger vehicle tires only. Rims must be removed. No large or off-road tires. Limit five tires per vehicle.

What materials are not accepted?
Appliances & large equipment

Air conditioners, refrigerators, stoves, ovens, water heaters, and other large household appliances.

Medical, radioactive & biohazardous waste

Medical waste, biological or infectious waste, syringes (sharps), asbestos, and radioactive materials.

Explosives & ammunition

Ammunition, explosives, and other explosive materials.

Commercial & industrial waste

Commercial or business-generated waste, 55-gallon drums, and other bulk waste materials.

Trash, recycling & non-HHW materials

Food waste, glass, metals, paper, newspapers, plastics, and solid waste intended for landfills.

Unaccepted tires

Large truck tires, oversized or off-road tires, and passenger tires with rims attached.

Do I need to be an Avondale resident?

Yes. HHW events are free for Avondale residents only. Proof of residency is required.

Can businesses participate?

No. HHW events are for residential waste only.

Do I need to make an appointment?

No appointment is required. HHW events are first-come, first-served.

What happens if I arrive late?

Materials will not be accepted after the event closing time. Arrive early to help ensure service.
