## Welcome to Our West Valley AZ

Our West Valley AZ is a community for neighbors across Arizona's West Valley to connect, share useful information, ask questions, help one another, and stay informed.

We serve Avondale, Goodyear, Buckeye, Litchfield Park, Tolleson, Glendale, Peoria, Surprise, and surrounding West Valley communities.

The website is designed as a quick reference for useful local information. Our goal is to make commonly needed information easier to find and public information easier to understand, while providing links to original sources when you want more detail.

Topics can include everyday city services, local government, budgets and spending, public safety, development, community issues, and other information affecting life in the West Valley.

Our Facebook and Nextdoor communities provide a place for neighbors to share local news and events, ask for recommendations, discuss community issues, talk about local businesses, help find missing pets, and connect with one another.

Different viewpoints are welcome. We encourage respectful discussion, good-faith participation, and helping one another get to accurate information.

Our goal is simple: help our neighbors connect, share, and better understand what's happening in the communities we call home.
